- 2026-11-11: Clarification of Section 5. Both key-frame exclusion rules are applied to the standard HR readout of Section 3, and only the listed bins are set to zero. The October code for this analysis also removed the window mean before the periodogram. This is not part of either rule; its effect on the development half is reported in the paper.
- 2026-11-11: Added src/st3/base.py, unchanged from commit 6ebbc9cd. Its embedded subject list equals the A split in lists/mmpd_subjects_A_B.csv. The evaluation is not changed.
- 2026-11-11: Added a sensitivity analysis to Section 5, fixed on MMPD-A (development data). On MMPD-B, recordings whose background shows a periodic peak near 2.0 Hz or 3.0 Hz are excluded, and C1–C4 are repeated. The background signal is the mean of the R, G and B values over the pixels outside the face box in each frame. The face box is the one used for the PhysNet input view: a fixed OpenCV detection on the first frame, enlarged as for that view and clipped to the frame. The spectrum is a Welch estimate on the 512-point HR grid at 30 fps: linear detrend of the whole signal, Hann window, 300-sample segments with 150-sample overlap, segment means removed. A recording is flagged when this spectrum has, in bins 33–35 (116.02–123.05 bpm) or 50–52 (175.78–182.81 bpm), a bin that is larger than both neighbours and at least twice the median of the six surrounding bins, three on each side. Recordings without background pixels are left out of this analysis. The implementation is src/stage5/mmpd_background_flags.py.
- 2026-11-13: Inference batching, random numbers and two sensitivity analyses. The evaluation of Sections 3 and 4 is not changed.
  1. Batching. In the inference code used for all arms, FactorizePhys outputs are standardized with the mean and standard deviation of the whole inference batch, as in the rPPG-Toolbox FactorizePhys test code (commit b7500b8). With FSAM enabled, its BVP head also uses the batch-wide minimum. FactorizePhys outputs therefore depend slightly on which chunks share a batch: in a development check on 30 MMPD-A windows, changing the batching changed the standardized waveform by up to 0.40 and the narrow-band HR readout of one window. PhysNet and RhythmFormer waveforms changed by at most 0.044, with no change in HR. The batching is fixed for all backbones and arms. MMPD-B: the chunks of the October input plan in its full order, four chunks per batch; batches may cross recording boundaries, and the last batch holds the remaining chunks. This is the batching used for MMPD-A, and the comparison of the re-inferred original-arm predictions with the stored October predictions (Section 3, Run) also verifies it. MCD-rPPG test half: per recording, chunks in temporal order, four chunks per batch; the last batch of a recording holds the remaining chunks. This is the batching used for the development half.
  2. Random numbers. The per-recording reset of the random-number state stays in place. In this code revision inference uses no random numbers: the FSAM basis is initialized with ones and dropout is inactive at evaluation. The reset therefore has no effect.
  3. FSAM. For FactorizePhys, the confirmatory contrasts are also reported with FSAM disabled at inference, as a sensitivity analysis.
  4. Line-removal input. No line-removal input procedure is fixed, so this sensitivity analysis of Section 5 is omitted. On the development half, RhythmFormer was re-inferred after removing, at each pixel, the mean deviation of each of the 12 frame phases of the key-frame interval from that pixel's overall mean. The key-frame-line fraction of high-HR windows fell by 0.012 to 0.071 across arms, and the contrasts changed by at most 0.53 bpm in high-HR MAE and by at most 1.12 bpm in low-HR MAE. This is reported as an exploratory result.
