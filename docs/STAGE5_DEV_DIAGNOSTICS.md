# Development-only diagnostics after the Stage 5 freeze

These analyses use development data (MMPD-A, the MCD-rPPG development half) or re-encoded data of UBFC-rPPG and of PURE subjects 09–10 only. They are never evaluated on the test sets. Their decision rules were written before any of their results, and this document is committed before any of them is run. All of them are exploratory. Rules 2 and 3 are run before the amendment that fixes the Stage 5 test run, so that the outcome of rule 2 can fix the line-removal input procedure of Section 5; the others may be completed after it. C2 denotes (ii-pc) − (ii) and C3 (i-pc) − (i), on high-HR MAE, paired by seed.

1. RhythmFormer trained with the negative Pearson loss only. Arms (ii) and (ii-pc), seeds 20261131–20261133; all other settings as in the standard RhythmFormer runs. C2 below is computed between these runs.
   - Convergence: if the high-HR MAE of (ii-pc) exceeds 1.5 times that of the standard (ii-pc) on either development set, no judgement is made.
   - Alignment response. If C2 has an upper bound below 0 on both development sets, we write that with the Pearson loss alone RhythmFormer gained from alignment, so that the absence of this gain under its standard loss depends on the loss as well as on the architecture. If the MCD-rPPG point estimate of C2 is at least +3 bpm, we write that the absence of an alignment gain does not depend on the loss. Otherwise the question is left open.
   - Key-frame line (high-HR windows of the MCD-rPPG development half). If the key-frame-line fraction of (ii-pc) is at most 0.05 in all three seeds, we write that the concentration at the key-frame frequency did not occur without the frequency loss. If it lies within or above the seed range of the standard (ii-pc) runs for at least two seeds, we write that it does not depend on the loss. Otherwise the question is left open.
2. Re-inference of RhythmFormer, arms (0), (i), (ii), (i-pc), (ii-pc), (ii-inv) and (ii-lag), on development inputs of MCD-rPPG low-pass filtered with a Gaussian of σ = 1, 1.5 and 2 pixels, applied to the 128×128 network input before normalization.
   - Judged at σ = 1.5 on arm (0), mean over seeds; the other arms and σ values are reported. A line window is a window read within 147.66–154.69 bpm by the same checkpoint with or without the filter.
   - If the key-frame-line fraction of high-HR windows falls by at least 50% (relative) and the high-HR MAE outside line windows changes by less than 1 bpm, we write that the attraction is consistent with a temporal modulation of spatial high-frequency content; if it falls by less than 20%, this explanation is rejected; otherwise it is left open.
   - If the first condition holds, filtering at σ = 1.5 becomes the line-removal input procedure of Section 5 for the RhythmFormer checkpoints on MCD-rPPG, fixed in the amendment that fixes the Stage 5 test run. Otherwise no line-removal input procedure is fixed.
3. Spectra of the mean absolute frame difference and of the Laplacian energy in the face view of the development videos: for MCD-rPPG, also folded at the key-frame interval; for MMPD-A, the values at 2.0 and 3.0 Hz compared between recordings flagged and not flagged by the background rule of the 2026-11-11 entry. Descriptive: a peak at the key-frame frequency supports a second-order (texture or noise) origin of the line.
4. Re-encoding of UBFC-rPPG and of PURE subjects 09–10 (not used for training) with the encoder identified in the MCD-rPPG bitstreams (MPEG-4 Part 2), the B-frame setting measured there (no B-frames) and a bit rate that gives the same number of bits per pixel as the MCD-rPPG development videos, with key-frame intervals of 10, 12 and 15 frames and two controls: without periodic key frames (interval longer than 300 frames, scene-cut key frames disabled), and the videos as distributed. Inference only.
   - Usability: the transplant is interpreted only if FactorizePhys (0) keeps, in every key-frame condition, at least half of its one-bin hit rate on the videos as distributed.
   - Prediction: RhythmFormer (0) predictions concentrate at fs/GOP (180, 150 and 120 bpm at 30 fps), and this concentration is absent in the controls and for the two convolutional backbones.
   - Windows whose reference HR lies within one bin of fs/GOP are excluded from the fractions below. High-HR contrasts are computed on UBFC-rPPG; PURE subjects 09–10 enter the fractions only.
   - We write that the concentration is induced by periodic key frames only if, for all three intervals, the fraction of windows read within one bin of fs/GOP is at least 0.2 for RhythmFormer (0) and at most 0.05 in both controls and for both convolutional backbones.
   - We write that the worsening of the aligned arms on MCD-rPPG is consistent with an interaction with compression artefacts only if (ii-pc) − (ii) and (i-pc) − (i) for RhythmFormer each have a lower bound above 0 in at least two of the three key-frame conditions and an interval including 0 in both controls.
   - The fractions at 120 and 180 bpm (intervals of 15 and 10 frames) are shown beside the MMPD-A fractions of windows read in the background bins of the 2026-11-11 entry (bins 33–35 and 50–52).
5. PhysNet trained with standardized labels (PhysNet-Std). Arms (ii), (ii-pc) and (ii-lag), seeds 20261131–20261133. The label is the standardized cumulative sum of the stored differenced label, which equals the standardized PPG label up to a shift of one sample; inputs and all other settings are as in the standard PhysNet runs.
   - Convergence: if the high-HR MAE of (ii-pc) exceeds 1.5 times that of the standard PhysNet (ii-pc) on either development set, no judgement is made.
   - Let D = [(ii-lag) − (ii)] of the standard runs minus [(ii-lag) − (ii)] of PhysNet-Std, paired by seed. If (ii-lag) − (ii) of PhysNet-Std has a point estimate of at most +1.0 bpm on MMPD-A, D has a lower bound above 0 on MMPD-A and D has a positive point estimate on the MCD-rPPG development half, we write that the larger high-HR error of (ii-lag) than of (ii) in PhysNet depended on the differenced label representation.
   - If (ii-lag) − (ii) of PhysNet-Std has a lower bound above 0 and a point estimate of at least +3 bpm on MMPD-A, we write that it did not depend on the label representation.
   - Otherwise the question is left open.
